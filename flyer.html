About the Alliance

Prairie Robotics Alliance, Inc. is dedicated to advancing STEM education through FIRST® Robotics programs and community partnerships in the Sun Prairie area.

Hands-on learning changes lives. By supporting student teams and community-facing STEM infrastructure, we open pathways into engineering, programming, and leadership — skills that translate directly into careers and impact.

We are building a multi-program pipeline from elementary school through high school graduation — and a community fab lab that will serve students and makers across eastern Dane County.
